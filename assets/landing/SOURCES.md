# Landing page pictures

The photographs are from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash License (free for
commercial use, no attribution required). Two of the feature cards are not
photographs but EstateCore UG itself, so that they show what the feature does:
the arrears card is the phone dashboard's "Who owes rent" list, and the receipt
card is a PDF receipt the app produced. Both were made from the example data
the local preview is seeded with; the tenants and amounts are invented.
